## What is a listing?

A listing refers to a fairly used items put up for sale by a decluttering business.

## What Does a Listing Contain?

### The list contains the following:
-  Name
-  Type
-  Description
-  Condition
-  Price
-  Weight
-  Dimension
-  Business (related_to)

## How the Seller/Business is identified
A listing is associated with a business. The business is identified independently of an account or user login.
Multiple listings associated with the same business share that business's identity. This allows the system to determine which listings belong to the same business without requiring seller accounts.

## What is explicitly out of scope?
-  Accounts
-  Images
-  Categories
-  Filters, Search
-  A sold state
